About This Invoice Generator

This tool creates professional UK invoices that comply with HMRC requirements for VAT invoices. All data is processed in your browser — nothing is sent to any server, and nothing is stored.

What a valid UK VAT invoice needs: your business name and address, VAT registration number, invoice number, invoice date, client details, description of goods/services, unit price and quantity, VAT rate and amount, and the total amount due.

Is this invoice legally valid?

Yes — UK invoices don't require a specific format. As long as they contain the required information (supplier details, client details, unique invoice number, date, description, amounts, and VAT details if registered), they're valid. This generator includes all required fields.

Do I need to charge VAT?

Only if you're VAT-registered (required when turnover exceeds £90,000). If you're not VAT-registered, set the VAT rate to 0% and leave the VAT number field blank. You must not charge VAT if you're not registered.

How long should I keep invoices?

HMRC requires you to keep records for at least 6 years (5 years from the 31 January submission deadline of the relevant tax year). If you're VAT-registered, keep VAT records for 6 years.

Is my data safe?

Yes. Everything runs in your browser. No data is sent to our servers or stored anywhere. When you close the page, the data is gone. We recommend downloading your PDF immediately after generating it.
