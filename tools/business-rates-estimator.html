How Business Rates Work

Business rates are calculated by multiplying your property's rateable value by the appropriate multiplier set by the government each year.

2025/26 multipliers: Standard multiplier: 55.5p in the pound. Small business multiplier: 49.9p (for properties with RV below £51,000).

Small Business Rate Relief: If your property's rateable value is £12,000 or below and it's your only business property, you get 100% relief (no rates to pay). Between £12,001 and £15,000, relief tapers from 100% to 0%.

How do I find my rateable value?

Search for your property on the Valuation Office Agency (VOA) website at gov.uk. Your rateable value is based on the open market rental value of your property as at 1 April 2021.

Can I appeal my rateable value?

Yes — you can challenge your rateable value through the VOA's Check, Challenge, Appeal process. You'll need evidence that the valuation is incorrect, such as comparable rental evidence.
